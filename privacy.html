Privacy Policy — ATOMverse

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Who we are

ATOMverse is developed by AkuDevStudio ("we", "us", "our"). Contact: akudevstudio@gmail.com

Data we collect

ATOMverse does not require an account and we do not run any servers. Your game progress is stored locally on your device. It leaves the device only if you choose to use Google Play Games cloud save (see below).

Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses Google Play Games Services (Google LLC) for achievements, a leaderboard (your largest reached scale) and optional cloud save. Google processes:

  • your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar),
  • unlocked achievements and leaderboard scores,
  • your saved game, only when you tap "Save to Google Play" in Settings,
  • diagnostics and analytics data collected by the Play Games SDK.

Leaderboard scores and achievements may be visible to other players according to your Play Games profile visibility settings. You can delete this data in your Play Games profile or your Google Account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show optional, rewarded video advertisements. AdMob (Google LLC) may collect and process data such as:

  • Advertising ID,
  • IP address and coarse location,
  • device and app usage information (ad interactions, diagnostics).

This data is processed by Google as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users in the EEA/UK are shown a consent form (Google UMP) before any ad-related data processing. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC) to understand how the game is played, for example sessions, retention, countries, devices and which achievements players reach. It may collect:

  • an app instance ID and the Advertising ID,
  • IP address and coarse location, device and app information,
  • in-game events (sessions, achievements unlocked, ad interactions).

Analytics is linked with our Google AdMob, Google Ads and Google Play accounts to measure how the game and its ads perform. User-level analytics data is deleted automatically after the retention period set in Google Analytics (at most 14 months).

In the EEA/UK, analytics starts only after you consent in the consent form, and you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

In-app purchases

ATOMverse contains no in-app purchases. We never ask for or process any payment information.

Legal bases and your rights (EEA/UK)

  • Personalised ads and analytics: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and Google Play Games features: our legitimate interest in running and funding a free game.

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). Write to akudevstudio@gmail.com to use these rights.

Children

ATOMverse is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are limited to content rated for teens.

Data retention & deletion

Gameplay data on the device can be deleted at any time via Settings → Reset All Data or by uninstalling the app. Data stored by Google Play Games (cloud save, achievements, scores) is deleted through your Play Games profile or Google Account, as described above.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date.

Contact
